## Prompt caméra (Runway / Kling)

Slow cinematic push-in along a stone arcade facade with potted citrus trees
in weathered terracotta urns, warm late-afternoon light casting long shadows
across the wall, archways and stone columns completely locked and static,
only the leaves of the citrus trees allowed a subtle natural sway, no
distortion on the architecture.

## Intention

domaine, lumière chaude de fin de journée, avant d'entrer dans l'intimité
des chambres.
