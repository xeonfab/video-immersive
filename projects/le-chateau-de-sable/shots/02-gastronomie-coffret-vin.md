# Plan 02 — La Cave (macro, coffret de vin)

**Catégorie** : gastronomie
**Photo source** : `photos-source/02-gastronomie-coffret-vin.jpg` — coffret
de vin (Brumont Montus La Tyre) et un verre posés sur un rocher de granit
brut
**Durée** : 4-5s

## Prompt caméra (Runway / Kling)

Very slow and subtle push-in on a wooden wine gift box and a glass resting on
a rough granite rock outdoors, the rock surface and the box completely locked
and static, no distortion on the label or the glass rim, soft natural
daylight, shallow depth of field with a gently blurred background, camera
holding an almost imperceptible drift forward only.
