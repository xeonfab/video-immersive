# Shot-list — Le Château de Sable

8 plans + outro. Positionnement : chillout haut de gamme, ancrage terroir
breton (granit, moutons noirs), gastronomie et bien-être. Voir
`selection-photos.md` pour le détail de la sélection des photos.

## Plan 01 — Gastronomie (dressage)

> Extremely slow and subtle push-in on a hand tilting a small ceramic pitcher
> and pouring a dark sauce over a plated dessert with white foam and red
> berries, the plate, the table and the hand's grip completely locked and
> static except for the pouring motion itself, shallow depth of field with a
> softly blurred green background, warm natural sunlight, no distortion on
> the plate rim or pitcher.

## Plan 02 — Gastronomie (coffret de vin)

> Very slow and subtle push-in on a wooden wine gift box and a glass resting
> on a rough granite rock outdoors, the rock surface and the box completely
> locked and static, no distortion on the label or the glass rim, soft
> natural daylight, shallow depth of field with a gently blurred background,
> camera holding an almost imperceptible drift forward only.

## Plan 03 — Chambre

> Very slow push-in inside a bright hotel room with a king size bed, soft
> natural light entering through the glazed veranda at the back, the walls,
> bed frame and window structure completely locked and static, no warping on
> the architectural lines, calm and quiet feel, gentle and minimal camera
> drift only.

## Plan 04 — Espaces communs (spa)

> Slow gentle pan across a spa treatment room with two massage tables
> covered in soft pink linen, the tiled walls and wall sconces completely
> locked and static, no distortion on the wall lines or the table edges,
> warm dim ambient lighting, soft focus on the linen texture, slow and
> minimal camera drift only.

## Plan 05 — Espaces communs (restaurant)

> Slow cinematic pan across an empty hotel restaurant room bathed in warm
> golden hour light, the chairs, tables and window frames completely locked
> and static, no distortion on the architectural lines, soft lens flare from
> the window light, gentle and minimal camera drift only.

## Plan 06 — Espaces communs (bar)

> Extremely slow overhead push-in on a flat-lay bar tray with a cocktail
> glass topped with fresh mint and a citrus slice, a tonic water bottle and
> a limoncello bottle, the tray, glass and bottles completely locked and
> static, no distortion on the bottle labels or glass rim, warm bar
> lighting, shallow depth of field, minimal and deliberate camera drift
> only.

## Plan 07 — Panorama (terrasse)

> Slow cinematic push-in from a hotel terrace overlooking a wide moorland
> landscape toward the horizon, the railing and deck structure completely
> locked and static, no distortion on the wooden planks or railing lines,
> bright natural daylight, gentle wind motion visible only in the distant
> grass, camera holding a slow and minimal push toward the horizon.

## Plan 08 — Paysage (territoire, moutons)

> Extremely slow and minimal push-in toward a group of black sheep grazing
> in a stone-walled meadow, the stone wall, wooden shelter and fence
> completely locked and static, no distortion on the walls or structures,
> natural bright daylight, camera motion confined almost entirely to the
> push-in itself — no attempt to track or follow the animals' own movement.
>
> ⚠️ Sujet vivant — risque de warping, voir `shots/08-*.md` pour le plan de
> repli.
