# Shot-list — Castel Beau Site (4 étoiles)

Perros-Guirec, Côte de Granit Rose. 5 plans + outro, prêts à coller dans
Runway Gen-3 / Kling.

| # | Plan | Durée | Prompt caméra |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | La Gastronomie (macro) | 4-5s | Macro shot of a hand slicing a ripened cheese wheel with a thin blade on a wooden board, extremely slow and deliberate motion, shallow depth of field, warm restaurant lighting, camera locked static with a very slight push-in, no distortion on the blade or board edges. |
| 02 | Le Panorama Côtier | 4-5s | Slow cinematic push-in from a hotel balcony overlooking a rocky cove and a distant château on the water, railing and balcony structure locked and static, gentle sea haze, golden hour light. |
| 03 | La Chambre Prestige | 4-5s | Very slow push-in inside a bright hotel room facing the sea through a glass veranda, soft natural light reflections on the floor, walls and window frame completely locked, no warping, calm and static feel. |
| 04 | Les Espaces Communs | 4-5s | Gentle pan across a hotel lounge library with a solid wood table, warm wood textures in focus, architectural lines and shelving locked, slow and minimal camera drift only. |
| 05 | Le Territoire | 4-5s | Slow push-in toward a lighthouse on pink granite rocks by the ocean, rock formations and lighthouse structure locked and static, ocean swell motion only in the water, overcast dramatic sky. |
| 06 | Outro de marque | ~2s | Fondu logo, voir `montage-edl.md` |
