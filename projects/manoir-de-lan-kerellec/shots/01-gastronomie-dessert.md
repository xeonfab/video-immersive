# Plan 01 — La Gastronomie (macro, dessert)

**Catégorie** : gastronomie
**Photo source** : `photos-source/01-gastronomie-dessert.jpg` — dessert gastronomique
raffiné (biscuit chocolat, mousse, gelée passion), main versant une sauce claire
depuis une timbale
**Durée** : 4-5s

## Prompt caméra (image-to-video)

Extremely slow and subtle push-in on an elegant plated dessert on a white
restaurant plate, a hand tilting a small white sauce jug and pouring a thin
steady stream of clear sauce onto the dish, precise and deliberate motion,
shallow depth of field with a softly blurred garden background, warm natural
restaurant lighting, camera locked static with only a very slight push-in, no
distortion on the plate, table edges, or the jug.

## Intention

Ouvrir la séquence sur le geste le plus sensoriel du dossier — un dressage de
dessert en train de se faire, pas un plat déjà posé. Capte l'attention en 2
secondes.

## Sound design

- **Ambiance** : "Very quiet close restaurant terrace room tone, faint
  distant garden birdsong, no music, intimate warm feel, near-silence with
  texture."
- **Foley** : "Thin stream of sauce being poured from a small ceramic jug
  onto a plate, light continuous trickle, close mic, no reverb."
